# ADR-0044 - M5-C5 economic computer-agent foundation

Economic observations compose C1-C4 immutable snapshots, while proposals
remain separate from simulation mutation. A deterministic bounded policy may
prioritize harvest, production, and structure interaction candidates, but it
does not spend credits, enqueue production, mutate cargo, occupy cells, or
create actors. Strategy labels are explicit project profiles, not original YR
AI compatibility claims. Neural/ONNX execution, rendering, gameplay, and
ProjectBaseline packed reads remain out of scope.
